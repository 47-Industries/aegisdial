# AegisDial — Positioning & Marketing Drafts (2026-04-19, rev 2)

**Positioning:** the only consumer fraud product that *prevents* AND *recovers*.
Every other product picks one. Truecaller, Hiya, RoboKiller, Apple Live Caller ID, carrier scam shields = prevention only. AARP fraud helpline + FTC reportfraud.ftc.gov = recovery only. Aura/LifeLock = identity insurance, not real-time prevention or guided recovery.

**The two-pillar story:**
- **Live Shield** — on-device AI listens for scam patterns mid-call and warns you before money moves.
- **Recovery Concierge** — AI guide for the first 15 minutes after a scam if one ever lands.

**The wedge isn't the story.** The wedge is the side-door distribution strategy: SEO + r/Scams + AARP partnerships lead with Recovery because that's where the high-intent victim search volume lives. But the front-door (App Store, landing page, paid ads, fundraise pitch) leads with the integrated **prevent + recover** moat.

ICP: adult children of aging parents (buyer) + 65+ scam victims and prevention-conscious 50+ (user).

## App Store — long description

**Stop the call. And if one ever lands, we walk you through it.**

Every other scam product makes you choose. Truecaller and Hiya block calls but leave you alone after the scam works. The AARP fraud helpline helps after — when you can get through. The FTC's report form is bureaucratic and cold.

AegisDial is the only product that does both — because no prevention is perfect, and recovery without prevention means you'll get hit again.

**Live Shield** *(Pro)* — On-device AI listens to suspicious calls in real time. The moment scam patterns appear — pressure tactics, gift-card requests, fake bank verifications — we warn you mid-call, before any money moves. Transcripts never leave your phone. Truecaller, Hiya, and Apple's own caller ID don't do this.

**Recovery Concierge** *(Pro or $99 once)* — Built for the moment after a scam. A trauma-informed AI guide walks you through the first 15 minutes — freezing accounts, calling your bank, locking your credit, filing FTC and IC3 reports, telling your family. 52 scam types covered, with playbooks written by fraud experts.

**Family Safe Words + Guardian Alerts** *(Pro)* — When the "grandchild emergency" call comes, your real grandchild knows the word. The scammer doesn't. Family members get an instant ping the moment a scam attempt is detected on a loved one's phone.

**Breach Monitor** *(Pro)* — Alerts when your email shows up in a new data breach.

**Free scam-text checker** — Got a sus text from "Chase" or "USPS"? Paste it in. We'll tell you if it's a scam and what the scammer was after. No signup, no payment.

**Pro: $49.99/mo or $299/yr — covers up to 3 family members. Family+ adds 2 more for $69.99/mo. Or $99 once for a single Recovery Session if you just need help with one event.**

Privacy: every word of your recovery session is end-to-end encrypted. Live Shield transcripts stay on your phone. We never sell data. Delete your account in two taps.

### Three-section body (each ~80 words, with screenshot)

**Section 1: "Live Shield catches scams while they're happening."**
On-device AI transcribes the call as it happens, matches against 1000+ scam phrase patterns, and warns you the second the scammer starts the script. Truecaller and Hiya warn before the call. We warn *during* — when the warning still matters. Transcripts never leave your phone.

**Section 2: "If one ever lands, Recovery Concierge takes you through it."**
The AARP helpline is at 1-877-908-3360. Wait time: minutes. The FTC's recovery form is a government webpage. Recovery Concierge is on your phone, instantly, with a step-by-step plan tailored to the exact scam — and an AI companion that knows what to say when you tell your spouse.

**Section 3: "Free scam-text checker. No signup."**
Got a weird text from "Chase"? Paste it. In two seconds you'll know if it's a known scam, what the scammer wanted, and whether you're already at risk. Use it for yourself, share it with your parents.
