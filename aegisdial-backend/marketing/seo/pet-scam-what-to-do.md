# Pet scam — online puppy or kitten that never arrived

A pet scam is heartbreaking because the loss isn't just money — you had a name picked out. You did nothing wrong except trust a too-polished website. Here's how to stop the compounding fees and recover what you can.

## What happens in this scam

You find an adorable puppy or kitten online at a price below comparable breeders, often from a site with stolen photos and a polished design. The "breeder" asks for a deposit by Zelle, Cash App, or wire. After you pay, a "transport company" charges extra for a crate, climate-controlled shipping, insurance, and customs. The pet never arrives, and each new fee opens the door to another.

## Why it worked on you (it's not your fault)

Attachment forms the moment you see the photo and name the pet. Fees feel like the cost of getting your new family member home, not the scam repeating. This is a business model — not a referendum on your judgment. Hundreds of these sites go up every week, and they're optimized for search results and emotion.

## What to do in the next 15 minutes

1. Stop paying. Every new fee — crate upgrade, customs, vaccination certificate, pet insurance, temperature-controlled shipping — is the same scam asking again.
2. If you wired money, call your bank immediately on the number from the back of your card. Wire recalls are possible in a narrow window.
3. If you used Zelle, ask the bank about the Zelle dispute process. If Cash App or PayPal, dispute in the app.
4. Screenshot everything: the listing, the breeder's site, every message, every receipt, every transport "invoice." You'll need these for filings.
5. Report the site and the scammer to the BBB Scam Tracker and file at ic3.gov.
6. File at reportfraud.ftc.gov.
7. If you gave ID or address info, watch for follow-on identity theft and consider a credit freeze.
8. Tell one friend or family member. Scammers will often come back with "we found your puppy, just one more fee."

## Red flags you might have missed (for next time)

- Price well below comparable breeders.
- Breeder refuses video calls of the specific animal.
- Payment demanded by Zelle, Cash App, wire, or gift card.
- Reverse image search shows the puppy's photo on many unrelated sites.
- Shipping fees compounding after the deposit.
