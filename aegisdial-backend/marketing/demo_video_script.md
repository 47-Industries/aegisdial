# AegisDial — 90-second demo video script (2026-04-19)

## Quick facts for the animator / videographer
- Duration: 90 seconds exact
- Aspect ratio: 9:16 (Instagram Reels / TikTok) with a 1:1 safe area for cross-posting to Facebook feed
- Voiceover: warm, adult-child-caring, not narrator-salesy. Female v/o, 40s, slight natural imperfection — think "daughter talking about her mom," not "brand spokesperson"
- Music: low-key piano under the scam beats, held tension chord during Live Shield, warmer strings resolve on recovery
- On-screen text: Inter SemiBold for UI, Inter Bold for scam alert; 24pt minimum so a 70-year-old can read it on a phone held in a lap
- Color palette: AegisDial navy + trust green (#1F9D55) on recovery; deep red (#C53030) only on the scam moment, nowhere else
- Devices shown: iPhone 16 (Mom), iPhone 16 Pro (daughter), nothing else. No Android, no laptops.
- Captions: burned-in, since 85% watch muted

## Full script

### Beat 1 — 0:00-0:05 — HOOK
**VISUAL:** Close on a woman, 68, at her kitchen table. Her iPhone is on speaker next to a half-finished crossword. She's leaning in, listening hard. We don't see the caller. Her hand hovers over a notepad where she's written a phone number and the word "fraud."
**VOICEOVER:** "Mom just got a call from her bank. At least, that's what they told her."
**ON-SCREEN TEXT:** "Mom. 2:14 PM. Tuesday."
**NOTES:** No music yet. Just ambient kitchen. The hook is her face.

### Beat 2 — 0:05-0:20 — THE PROBLEM
**VISUAL:** Over-the-shoulder on her phone screen — incoming call labeled "Wells Fargo Fraud Dept." Cut to her face as she nods. Cut to the notepad: she's writing "move money — safe account — gift cards." Cut back to her — she swallows. Piano enters, slow.
**VOICEOVER:** "They knew her last four. They said someone inside the bank was stealing. Don't tell your family. Drive to CVS. Buy the cards."
**ON-SCREEN TEXT:** 0:08 — "They knew her account number."  |  0:13 — "Don't tell anyone."  |  0:17 — "Go to the Bitcoin ATM."
**NOTES:** The phrases are real — pulled from FTC Consumer Sentinel 2024 playbooks. Pace the three text cards to hit on each pressure line.

### Beat 3 — 0:20-0:40 — LIVE SHIELD
**VISUAL:** Her phone screen fills the frame. Mid-call, the AegisDial overlay slides down from the top — deep red banner, white text: "This is a scam. Hang up now." Under it, a quieter line: "They said 'move your money to a safe account.' Banks never say this." A single big red button: "End Call." Her thumb taps it. Screen goes to home screen. Cut to her exhaling, hand on chest. Piano resolves one note warmer.
**VOICEOVER:** "AegisDial listens for the exact lines scammers use. When it hears one, it stops her, mid-call, before the money moves."
**ON-SCREEN TEXT:** 0:24 (inside the app banner) — "SCAM DETECTED — Bank Impersonation"  |  0:32 — "$0 lost."  |  0:37 — "Call ended in 3 seconds."
**NOTES:** The red banner must be the only red in the whole video up to this point. The app UI shown is the real Live Shield screen — get it from the build, don't redesign.

### Beat 4 — 0:40-0:55 — THE OTHER HALF
**VISUAL:** Hard cut. Different room, dimmer. A man, 72, sitting on a couch, phone face-down beside him, staring at nothing. A bank statement is on the coffee table. Cut to his daughter, 40s, sitting next to him, her phone open to AegisDial's Recovery Concierge. On her screen: a step card reading "Step 1 of 7 — Freeze the account." A chat bubble below: "I'll stay with you. Want me to dial the bank's fraud line for you?"
**VOICEOVER:** "But no filter catches every call. When one lands, AegisDial walks you through the next seven hours."
**ON-SCREEN TEXT:** 0:44 — "He already paid."  |  0:49 — "Recovery Concierge"  |  0:52 — "Freeze. Report. Recover."
**NOTES:** Lighting shift is the story beat — warm kitchen to cool living room. Don't let the father look pathetic; he looks tired. The daughter's phone is the hero prop here.

### Beat 5 — 0:55-1:20 (1:20 = 80s mark) — THE TWIN PILLARS
**VISUAL:** Split screen, vertical. Left: Mom's phone with the Live Shield red banner from Beat 3, frozen. Right: the daughter's phone scrolling through Recovery Concierge steps — "Call Chase fraud line," "File FTC report" (pre-filled form visible), "Tell your kids," each checking off green as we watch. Beneath both screens, a thin line of copy animates in.
**VOICEOVER:** "Every other app makes you pick. Truecaller blocks the call. AARP helps after. AegisDial is the only one that does both — because no prevention is perfect."
**ON-SCREEN TEXT:** 1:00 — "Prevent."  (left side)  |  1:02 — "Recover."  (right side)  |  1:10 — "Only AegisDial does both."  |  1:16 — "Americans lost $10B+ to fraud last year. — FTC, 2024"
**NOTES:** The pre-filled FTC form on the right must show real FTC field labels (ReportFraud.ftc.gov). The $10B stat is the only number we cite — don't add more.

### Beat 6 — 1:20-1:30 (80s-90s mark) — CTA
**VISUAL:** Full-screen AegisDial logo on navy. Below it, an App Store badge. Tight pricing bug in the lower third: "$49.99/mo  ·  $99 one-time  ·  Free scam-text checker, no signup." Final frame holds for 2 seconds on the logo alone.
**VOICEOVER:** "Stop the call. And if one ever lands, we'll walk you through it. AegisDial. On the App Store."
**ON-SCREEN TEXT:** 1:22 — "AegisDial"  |  1:25 — "On the App Store"  |  1:28 — "aegisdial.com"
**NOTES:** Logo animates in, doesn't cut. Pricing bug is small — don't let it dominate the frame.

## Production notes
- Scam phrases to surface in Live Shield warnings (all drawn from SCAM_MECHANICS playbooks — real, not invented):
  - "Move your money to a safe account"
  - "Go to the Bitcoin ATM"
  - "Don't tell anyone you're doing this"
  - "There's a warrant for your arrest"
  - "Your Social Security number has been suspended"
- Recovery Concierge screens to hero:
  - Step card (Step 1 of 7, "Freeze the account")
  - Companion chat bubble ("I'll stay with you")
  - Pre-filled FTC ReportFraud.ftc.gov form preview (fields: type of fraud, amount, payment method, contact method — these are the real FTC fields)
- Only verifiable stat used: FTC Consumer Sentinel 2024 — $10B+ lost to fraud. No other numbers.
- Muted viewing: every beat's on-screen text alone must tell the story. Read the text cards aloud in order — if they don't carry it, rewrite.
