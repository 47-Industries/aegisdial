Frequently asked

Does AegisDial record my calls?

No. Live Shield uses Apple's on-device speech recognizer. Audio is processed on your phone and never transmitted or stored.

Is there a free trial?

No. Scams are a psychological event, not a utility. Free users churn. We'd rather you pay $49.99 and stay protected for seven years than try us for a week and forget.

Why not Truecaller or Hiya?

Those services identify callers. AegisDial identifies SCRIPTS — the actual language a scammer uses. Very different.

Can I cancel anytime?

Yes. iPhone Settings → Apple ID → Subscriptions → AegisDial → Cancel. Takes 10 seconds.
